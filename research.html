Working paper

Withdrawal Fees, Information Design, and Bank-Run Fragility

Read abstract +

I study demand-sensitive withdrawal fees in a deposit contract and the trade-offs for a regulator when choosing the fee level and disclosing its history. Fees can reduce panic runs and improve liquidity provision unless banks offset them by overpromising short-term liquidity. Threshold-based flat fees can trigger preemptive runs, but may dominate continuous fees when fee movements reveal market stress and transparency is mandatory. If depositors learn that fees can be positive also under good fundamentals, these information effects are contained. When liquidity access is prioritized, fees go to zero; when stability is prioritized, fees may reach their maximum and disclosure becomes opaque.
