01.1

A little context

I am interested in the simplified models people build to understand the world, and what happens when those models shape markets and politics.

I combine economic theory and empirical methods with ideas from psychology and anthropology. My work ranges from bank runs and information disclosure to narratives, culture, and historical financial records.

I graduated with honours from Bocconi University’s MSc in Economic and Social Sciences. My research experience includes work at Bocconi’s CLEAN unit, a project on Renaissance Florentine banking, and policy analysis at the Osservatorio sui Conti Pubblici Italiani.
